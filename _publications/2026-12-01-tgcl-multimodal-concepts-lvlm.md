**NeurIPS 2026 Spotlight.** We introduce Text-Guided Concept Learning (TGCL), a weakly supervised method for extracting concept vectors from large vision–language models. TGCL probes patch-level activations guided by text concepts and separates concept patches from background through a contrastive, linear disentanglement step — no token-level annotations required.

On ImageNet and MSCOCO, TGCL improves over baselines in sparsity, stability, overlap, attribution alignment, and faithfulness.
